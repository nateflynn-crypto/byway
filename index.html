The long way, on purpose

Byway

Turn the miles between here and there into a string of oddities, landmarks, waterfalls and tiny museums just off the road.

    5 mi

    There's almost always something good a few miles off the highway.

    Add a start and a destination (and any stops in between). Byway draws the drive, scans a corridor along it, and builds a spread-out shortlist so one big city can't crowd out the rest of the trip.

    How it works

    Routing comes from OSRM. Byway then asks OpenStreetMap (through the Overpass API) for named attractions, museums, landmarks, historic sites, public art, viewpoints, waterfalls, covered bridges and more inside your corridor. Places linked to Wikipedia get a description and photo.

    Distances are straight-line from the route, and detour times are rough estimates. Always check hours and access before you leave the highway.
